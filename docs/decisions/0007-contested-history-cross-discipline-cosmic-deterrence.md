# ADR-0007: Contested history, interoperable discoveries and transcendent deterrence

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-04.

The setting incorporates source-informed original historical accounts, conflicting beliefs, cross-cultural scientific and supernatural discoveries, and high-power entities whose interventions are limited by competing interests and explicit costs. Claims from online conspiracy cultures are modeled as claims, not automatically true statements about real people. Timeline modification is deferred until versioned causal branching is designed.

See [design specification](../world/contested-history-and-cosmic-politics.md).
