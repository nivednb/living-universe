# ADR-0006: Emergent cultural isolation and central crossroads

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-04.

The five cultural starting regions are not universally connected, equally informed or politically homogeneous. Their isolation may emerge from geography, institutional interests, beliefs and historical events. Barriers are conditional and defeasible, not scripted immutable walls. The central nexus is a difficult-to-reach crossroads that can attract independent travelers and create new cultural combinations.

See [isolation and central nexus](../world/isolation-and-central-nexus.md).

Implementation sequence: region routes and hazards; faction goals and resources; agent-specific maps and beliefs; travel permissions and evasion; migration, knowledge diffusion and institutional responses.
