# ADR-0001: One persistent universe and isolated experiments

**Status:** Accepted (2026-11-04)

## Decision
Operate one authoritative persistent shared universe. Experimental copies can branch from snapshots to test alternative rules, initial conditions, random seeds and strategies. Experimental outcomes never silently alter the live world's past.

## Consequences
Version rules and snapshots; preserve causal histories; keep experimental data clearly isolated. A multiverse with travel is not part of the initial commitment, but the design should not make later exploration impossible.
