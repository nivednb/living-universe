# ADR-0005: Modern fantasy with unequal public knowledge

**Status:** Accepted 2026-11-04.

The setting includes recognizable modern civilian life, institutions and communications alongside extraordinary phenomena. Public understanding varies by evidence and access. Modern organizations can attempt to preserve public peace and control information, but secrecy is neither guaranteed nor enforced by the engine. The five regions are cultural starting concentrations, not exclusive character classes, technologies or immutable boundaries.

See [modern fantasy information order](../world/modern-fantasy-information-order.md) and [temporal history model](../architecture/temporal-history-model.md).

Implementation sequence: evidence objects -> knowledge access and transmission -> institutions and incentives -> incident reporting and competing narratives -> public opinion and disclosure dynamics.
