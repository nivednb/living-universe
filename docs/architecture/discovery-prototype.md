# Cross-tradition discovery prototype

`prototype/discovery.py` models prerequisite-based research, tradition-specific expertise and direct teaching. A researcher familiar with scientific calculus and cultivation techniques may discover a new technique if prerequisites and expertise are sufficient. Discovery is recorded with the prerequisite knowledge IDs; knowledge is not automatically shared with other agents.

This is a **standalone deterministic reference module**, not an autonomous research simulation. It does not yet model experimental uncertainty, research time, physical equipment, social trust, licensing, resource costs, secrecy, failed hypotheses, provenance from external literature, or integration with the authoritative scheduler and checkpoint format.

Next steps: historical event integration, research scheduling, evidence and hypothesis graphs, resource costs, and tests for save/restore continuity.
