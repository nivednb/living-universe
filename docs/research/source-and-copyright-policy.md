# Research sources, copyright and provenance policy

**Status:** Adopted research policy. This is project guidance, not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Objective
Build a culturally rich, original world from historical evidence, public-domain material, folklore, scholarship and analysis of fiction across languages. **Free-to-read does not mean free-to-copy or redistribute.**

## Source tiers
1. **Public domain, CC0, or explicitly compatible licensed materials:** may be adapted within their actual terms; verify jurisdiction, edition, translations, illustrations and attribution.
2. **Historical and factual references:** extract verifiable facts, chronology, broad practices and citations; do not copy modern protected prose, translations, photographs or database collections.
3. **Copyrighted novels, comics, games and films (including free reading sites):** use only as inspiration for unprotected ideas, genre conventions and comparative analysis. Do not ingest, scrape, bulk store, reproduce, paraphrase plots as substitute stories, or copy distinctive characters, dialogue, world lore or visual designs without permission.
4. **Uncertain sources:** record links and metadata for review; no content ingestion or derivative adaptation until rights are clarified.

## Provenance ledger
For each seed or design mechanic track: source URL and title, author, publication/edition, language, license/permission, fact-versus-fiction classification, transformed original mechanic, and attribution obligation. Respect site terms, robots restrictions and privacy rights. Public accessibility alone does not authorize training or data mining; check relevant rights and exceptions before any bulk processing.

## Creative independence
Use multiple influences to develop original mechanisms, history and culture. Changing names or superficial details does **not** cure infringement if substantial protected expression is retained. Never present invented histories as authentic human history. Avoid treating fictional works as evidence of real cultural practice.

## Distribution
The project intends to allow downstream commercial and noncommercial reuse of its **original** components. Keep incompatible noncommercial or otherwise restricted content out of the redistributable core. Asset and dependency licenses are audited individually.
