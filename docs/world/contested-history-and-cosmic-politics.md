# Contested history, cross-discipline discovery and cosmic deterrence

**Status:** Design direction accepted 2026-11-04. Not yet implemented as autonomous systems.

## Historical source ecology
Use mythology, public-domain literature, licensed resources, academic history, folklore, and online conspiracy narratives as **research inputs**, never as unquestioned facts. For contemporary real-world conspiracy allegations about identifiable people, preserve the distinction between documented events, unverified claims, falsehoods and fictional adaptation. Prefer original fictional characters and institutions; avoid copying copyrighted plotlines or inventing defamatory factual assertions.

Every seed should track: source title, original language, date/edition, URL or bibliography, license/reuse permission, factual status, transformation notes, and whether it is a fictional world-truth event, an in-world belief, an unreliable account or an openly fictional text. Competing accounts may coexist without altering authoritative events.

## Fictional mirrors of ordinary humanity
In-world scholars may study stories of a seemingly powerless technological civilization resembling modern Earth. Some may interpret those stories as fiction, parallel-world observations or propaganda. This is an original in-world cultural motif, not an assertion that real-world conspiracies are true.

## Shared mechanisms, different explanatory frameworks
Scientific, magical, cultivation, primal and innate-power traditions can observe overlapping phenomena using different concepts and tools. Discoveries require observations, models, training, experiments, resources and social transmission. Combining disciplines can unlock new hypotheses or methods but must not automatically produce unlimited power. Track provenance, prerequisites, costs, failures, reproducibility and externalities.

Examples: numerical optimization improves a cultivator's energy control; cultivation observation inspires an instrument; a mage and scientist co-develop a new measurement method.

## Transcendent actors and cosmic deterrence
Some entities may be capable of extreme temporal, spatial or causal interventions, subject to world-specific costs, constraints, detection, uncertainty and countermeasures. Multiple factions can deter one another, but deterrence can fail. Interventions are decisions made by bounded agents, not arbitrary authorial rescue. Their personal relationships, obligations, ideologies and imperfect intelligence affect action.

**Causal consistency:** Time reversal, alternate histories and reality editing require explicit versioned timeline semantics before implementation. Never silently rewrite the authoritative historical ledger or checkpoints. Initial implementation should represent interventions as proposed actions with costs and effects, without changing the past.

## Simulation milestones
1. Source/provenance schema and evidence confidence.
2. In-world contradictory claims and publication channels.
3. Discovery graph with cross-tradition prerequisites and experiments.
4. Institutional monopolies and reactions to discoveries.
5. High-power actor goals, intervention thresholds and deterrence.
6. Explicit timeline branching/rollback semantics only after deterministic replay and checkpointing are verified.
