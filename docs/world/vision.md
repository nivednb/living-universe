# World vision

## Core commitment
One persistent, autonomous world whose history continues independently of player participation. The world is not organized around a chosen hero or scripted winner.

## Geography
Five starting cultures—magic, cultivation/immortality, science, primal, and inborn superpowers—surround a central meeting island. These are historical and educational orientations, not permanent character classes or species restrictions.

## Characters
Individual agents can work, travel, research, form relationships, inherit, migrate, govern, compete, cooperate, create institutions and change the environment. Players may enter the same world through avatars or observe without participating.

## Time and history
Long-lived beings may accumulate centuries of knowledge and achieve rare breakthroughs. Generations, inheritance, historical documents, ruins, lost knowledge and environmental transformations persist. Replays must use recorded events rather than invented past interactions.

## Interface
An observer/research dashboard and a top-down 2D multiplayer RPG are clients of the same authoritative world.
