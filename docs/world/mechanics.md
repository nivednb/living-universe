# Shared world mechanics

## One universe, multiple phenomena
Matter, air, mana, qi and other fields coexist. A character's perception depends on biology, knowledge, training, equipment and environmental conditions—not nationality. Phenomena can interact when permitted by the underlying rules.

## Development and fairness
Effort, inherited traits, opportunity, knowledge, experience, resources and luck apply to everyone. Effort causes meaningful state changes without guaranteeing identical outcomes. Characters can study and combine cultivation, magic, technology, innate powers and unfamiliar traditions.

## Discovery
Distinguish ground-truth mechanics, individual beliefs, transmitted knowledge and surviving records. Techniques and discoveries have prerequisites, mechanisms, costs, failure modes, evidence and provenance. Ancient discoveries can be rediscovered, tested and combined. Breakthroughs may arise after very long accumulation and insight rather than from a timer or XP threshold.

## Environmental consequences
Damage, creation, repair and transformation affect authoritative world state. Use multiscale models for buildings, cities, terrain, ecosystems and potentially planetary effects; record consequences and history without simulating every particle.

## Configurability
Keep the live universe's foundational rules stable and versioned. Allow in-world discoveries and institutions to evolve. Test alternative foundational laws only in isolated experimental copies.
